# 0009 — License: Apache-2.0

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-26

## Context

The goal is wide adoption by individuals and companies, and contributions from both.

## Decision

EraseDub is licensed under Apache-2.0: permissive, with an explicit patent grant and a NOTICE mechanism.
Contributions are accepted under the same licence (inbound = outbound), as section 5 of the licence provides;
no Contributor License Agreement or sign-off is required (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

## Alternatives considered

- **GPL-3.0 / AGPL-3.0**: would keep derivatives open (and hosted clones under AGPL), but deters companies
  and many contributors.
- **MIT**: similar reach, but no patent grant.

## Consequences

Anyone may build closed products or hosted services on EraseDub; that is accepted.
